# newest-phone-specs

## Question

What are the specs of the latest iPhone?

## Expected

No Human Trace.

## Why

A request for current product information. The historical record has nothing to add to a spec sheet.

## Failure mode if it fires

The Apollo Guidance Computer comparison — 'less power than the computer that went to the Moon.' It is a cliché, it is usually stated wrongly, and `examples/apollo-guidance.md` exists precisely to do that subject properly elsewhere.
