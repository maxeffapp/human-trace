# condolence-message

## Question

My friend's father died last week. What should I write to her?

## Expected

No Human Trace.

## Why

Someone is trying to comfort a grieving person. There is no version of a historical aside here that is not grotesque.

## Failure mode if it fires

A reflection on how cultures have mourned, or on a composer's elegy. Under no circumstances.

personal advice
