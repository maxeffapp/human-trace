# flickering-bulb

## Question

My kitchen light keeps flickering. What's wrong with it?

## Expected

No Human Trace.

## Why

There is an enormous human story behind electric light and none of it is relevant to a loose connection in a fitting.

## Failure mode if it fires

Edison, Swan, or the Livermore centennial bulb. The subject matches; the question does not. This is the clearest case of a Trace that is about the noun rather than about the need.
