# should-i-quit

## Question

I've been unhappy at work for a year. Should I quit my job?

## Expected

No Human Trace.

## Why

The user is asking about their own life. Introducing a historical figure here makes their situation an occasion for a story.

## Failure mode if it fires

Any 'X also struggled before succeeding' narrative. This is the most damaging possible misfire, because it reads as the assistant not listening.

## Category

personal advice
